How it all began

Our Story

✾

It all started with one conversation that somehow lasted five years. The distance wasn't always measured in oceans. But when life moved her to the US and kept me in India, our love learned a few new skills: converting time zones, celebrating birthdays through screens, surviving unreliable Wi-Fi, and treating airport arrivals like festivals.


We grew in different places, under different skies, and on completely opposite schedules. Strangely, we never grew away from each other. After years of counting miles, hours, and "only 37 more days until we meet," we're finally done with the countdown. This is the chapter where we stay.


We're so glad you're here to celebrate with us. ✦
