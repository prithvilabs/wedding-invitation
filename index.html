🎊 Celebrations

The Wedding Events

✨
Day Before · Jan 27
Mehendi & Sangeet 💛
An evening of music, dance, and beautiful mehndi designs. Come celebrate with song and laughter as we welcome the families together.
Wedding Day · Jan 28
Nischayathartham 🪷
The sacred engagement ceremony where both families formally agree to the union with the blessings of elders.
Wedding Day · Jan 28
Muhurtham — The Sacred Knot 🌺
The auspicious moment of the Kasi Yatra, Maalai Maathal, and Tying of the Thali. Witness the sacred Tamil wedding rituals at their most beautiful.
Evening · Jan 28
Wedding Reception 🎶
An evening of celebration, grand feast, music, and dancing. Bless the newlyweds as they begin their journey together.
